## 2. Marketing Channel & Attribution Strategy

### Finding 1: The Paid Social "Introducer" Dilemma
- **Empirical Observation:** Meta Ads (Paid Social) accounts for 32% of total traffic and delivers ₹2.65M in First-Touch attributed revenue, but only ₹1.82M under Last-Touch attribution (-₹830k variance).
- **Strategic Implication:** A naive marketing team looking solely at Last-Touch ROAS (0.88x) would erroneously cut Meta spend. In reality, Paid Social is the primary discovery vehicle that introduces prospective customers who later convert through Direct, Email, or Organic Search.
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Evaluate Paid Social performance on a **blended Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)** and First-Touch ROAS rather than Last-Touch platform ROAS.
  2. Implement dedicated mid-funnel creative (video customer testimonials, fabric technology explainers) to shorten the window from discovery to first conversion.

### Finding 2: Email Marketing as a High-Margin Closing Engine
- **Empirical Observation:** Email delivers exceptional conversion rates (8.5%–11.0%) and achieves an outstanding ROAS exceeding 15x due to minimal marginal delivery costs. Under Last-Touch attribution, Email gains +₹420k in attributed revenue compared to First-Touch.
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Expand automated lifecycle journeys: deploy a 3-part **Abandoned Cart Recovery Flow** with dynamic product previews, and a 45-day **Replenishment Flow** for consumable activewear staples (sports bras and everyday tanks).
  2. Implement segmented messaging based on RFM tiers rather than generic brand blasts.

### Finding 3: Google Ads (Search & Shopping) Delivers High-Intent Conversions
- **Empirical Observation:** Google Brand Search maintains a high CTR (6.5%) and low CPC (₹14), while Performance Max (PMax) captures high-intent shopping queries across Tier-1 metros.
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Protect brand defense keywords to prevent competitor conquesting.
  2. Expand PMax product asset groups to highlight high-margin leggings and studio flared pants.

## 3. Product Portfolio & Merchandise Economics

### Finding 4: Hero SKUs Drive Category Concentration
- **Empirical Observation:** Two categories—**Leggings** (₹2.84M, 34.6% share) and **Sports Bras** (₹1.72M, 21.0% share)—drive over 55% of total commercial revenue.
- **Flagship SKU:** The **Contour Seamless Leggings (P001)** is the single bestselling item in the portfolio, generating ₹1.35M in sales with a realized gross margin of 66.8%.
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Anchor top-of-funnel ad creative to the Contour Seamless Leggings and CloudFlex Sports Bra as entry-point "gateway" products.
  2. Build high-converting product bundles (e.g., "Leggings + Sports Bra Matching Set") to increase AOV beyond ₹4,500.

### Finding 5: Promotional Discount Discipline
- **Empirical Observation:**
  - Full-Price Orders (0% discount) generate **45.2% of total revenue** at a **67.4% gross margin**.
  - Moderate Discounts (11–20%) generate 28.5% of revenue at a 61.2% margin.
  - Clearance Discounts (>20%) erode gross margin down to **52.6%**.
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Avoid sitewide blanket discount sales, which train consumers to delay full-price purchases.
  2. Restrict promotional discounts (>20%) strictly to end-of-season clearance SKUs with sluggish inventory turnover.

## 4. Customer Retention & RFM Lifetime Value

### Finding 6: VIP Pareto Principle
- **Empirical Observation:** **VIP Champions** represent **12.4% of total buyers** but generate **34.8% of all commercial revenue** (average spend ₹11,240 vs portfolio average ₹5,935).
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Create a dedicated **GrowthIQ VIP Club**: provide early 24-hour access to new product drops, free priority shipping, and bespoke anniversary gifts.
  2. Ensure customer service tickets from VIP customers are prioritized with sub-1-hour SLA resolution.

### Finding 7: At-Risk High Value Reactivation Opportunity
- **Empirical Observation:** **148 customers** are classified as **At-Risk High Value** (recency > 180 days, but historical spend > ₹7,500). Total historical spend in this cohort exceeds ₹1.2M.
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Launch an automated "We Miss You" winback series offering a tiered credit (e.g., "₹500 off your next ₹2,500 order") rather than a percentage discount.
  2. Solicit feedback via a one-click micro-survey to diagnose reasons for lapse (sizing issues, fabric wear, delivery delays).

## 5. Conversion Funnel Optimization

### Finding 8: The Add-to-Cart Drop-Off Bottleneck
- **Empirical Observation:** 58.2% of sessions view a product detail page, but only 21.8% add an item to their cart (a 62.5% step drop-off). Once in the cart, 44.5% proceed to checkout.
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Introduce an interactive **Size & Fit Recommender** directly above the Add-to-Cart CTA to reduce fit hesitation.
  2. Surface social proof badges ("Over 2,500 5-star reviews") and localized delivery estimates ("Delivers to Mumbai in 2 days").

### Finding 9: Mobile UX Friction
- **Empirical Observation:** Mobile devices account for 74% of traffic but convert at **2.85%**, whereas Desktop converts at **4.12%**.
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Streamline mobile checkout by implementing native **1-Click UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)** and Apple Pay.
  2. Remove non-essential form fields on mobile checkout pages.

## 6. Supply Chain & Inventory Risk Mitigation

### Finding 10: Critical Stockout Vulnerability
- **Empirical Observation:** The inventory snapshot reveals that **P001 (Contour Seamless Leggings)** and **P006 (CloudFlex Sports Bra)** have fallen below safety stock thresholds (18–32 units on hand against reorder points of 50 units with 14-day vendor lead times).
- **Commercial Risk:** Running out of hero SKUs directly impairs acquisition efficiency, as paid ads driving to out-of-stock sizes waste media spend.
- **Actionable Recommendation:**
  1. Issue immediate purchase orders for 100 units of P001 and 75 units of P006.
  2. Implement an automated inventory alert webhook alerting the merchandising team whenever stock drops below 1.5x lead-time demand.
